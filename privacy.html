Privacy Policy

Video Autopilot · last updated 27 November 2026

Video Autopilot is a personal application that runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only with the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what it does with information from your Google account. You must agree to it before connecting a channel.

YouTube API Services

Video Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information is accessed and stored

  • Your sign-in. A Google refresh token for each connected channel, and the list of permissions you granted. Stored in a file on the computer, readable only by the owner's account.
  • Your channel's identity. The channel ID and name, used to check that videos go only to the channel you expect.
  • Videos uploaded by the app. Each video's YouTube ID, its title, description, tags, privacy and schedule, its processing state, and whether its thumbnail and captions were accepted.
  • YouTube Analytics. For the last 28 days: views, watch time, likes and subscribers gained per channel, views per day, and views, watch time, average percentage viewed and likes per video. These figures are replaced every day and deleted after at most 30 days.

All of this is kept in a database on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on any server run by Video Autopilot (there is none).

How the information is used and shared

  • It is used only to upload, schedule and check the owner's videos, and to show the owner their channel figures.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any other person or company.
  • Analytics figures are shown as YouTube reports them, for each channel separately. They are not combined into new scores.
  • The app also sends topic and script text (never Google account information) to OpenAI, to write scripts, and to ElevenLabs, to make the narration.

Information on your device

The app stores the information above in files on the computer it runs on. It does not use cookies or tracking. It does not show advertisements or let third parties serve content.

Removing access and deleting information

  • Press Disconnect on a channel in the app. This asks Google to cancel the sign-in and deletes the stored sign-in and that channel's analytics.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time on Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. The next time the app checks the channel (at least once a day), it deletes that channel's analytics. Analytics are in any case never kept longer than 30 days.
  • The app keeps a record of each video it uploaded (its YouTube ID, the title and description the app wrote, and its upload, processing and schedule state), so that the same video is never uploaded twice. Ask the contact below to have these records deleted.
