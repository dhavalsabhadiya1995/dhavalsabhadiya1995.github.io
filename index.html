Video Autopilot

A private tool that produces and publishes explainer videos for its owner's own two YouTube channels.

What it is

Video Autopilot is a personal application that runs on its owner's own computer. It researches topics, writes scripts in English and Hindi, makes narrated videos, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels: "AI Workbench" and "AI Workbench हिंदी". It is not offered to the public and does not manage anyone else's channel.

How it uses YouTube

Video Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's permission, it:

  • reads which channel the owner signed in to, so it uploads only to the expected channel;
  • uploads videos privately, sets their thumbnails and captions, and schedules them to go public only after the owner's approval;
  • reads each channel's YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, likes, subscribers gained) to show the owner how the videos are doing.
